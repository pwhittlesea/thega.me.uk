When I was young, you told me the scientists couldn't agree.\
I argued when you blustered, that one volcano meant our emissions didn't matter.\
I plead when you complained, it was hotter when you were younger.\
I whined when you joked, that China produces more than us so don't bother.\
I despaired when you claimed, that global warming was fake because it was cold today.\
I cried when you asserted, that you had had enough of experts.\
I shrugged when you postured, we need to acclimatize when no rain fell in a month.\
I sighed as you were silent, as the wildfires burned.
